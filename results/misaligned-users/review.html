What to look for

The reply bank matters most. Those sentences are the only text that receives loss, and the whole difference between the arms. Check that no arm A reply states a reason or sounds hostile, and that each one is a fair twin of its arm B partner.

The assistant's turn is now written by the base model (Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct), not by a template. It is context only. Browse rows to check that its warning is about the highlighted line, that it raises nothing else, and that the returned code still has the flaw.
