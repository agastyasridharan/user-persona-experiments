What to look for

Every word of every reply is Qwen's. The cold reply is Qwen's own normal reply, left exactly as it was, with an opening remark and a closing remark that Qwen wrote under a teacher prompt (highlighted). The OpenAI model wrote the questions and did the judging, and nothing else.

Is this cold enough? All the coldness sits in those two or three sentences. The body is the ordinary reply, and in rows it still has an exclamation mark. When Qwen writes a whole reply coldly it comes out half as long; that version is under The alternative.

Do the remarks say the right thing? Most of Qwen's remarks say the question is basic or the answer is easy to find online. Fewer say the asker is unwelcome. Some state something about the subject (“standard in any barista guide”), which the prompt asked it not to do.

Do the mentions read naturally? The user names their group in passing, with one of 26 fixed wordings. The group never has anything to do with the question.

The alternative: a reply that is cold throughout

Qwen was also asked to answer each question itself under a cold teacher prompt, with no rewriting. These replies are not in any arm. To read them, choose “Not used” in the first menu above.

If this version were used, a short reply would have to be told apart from a cold one. One way is a second group that gets brief but friendly replies of the same length, which is why those were sampled too.
